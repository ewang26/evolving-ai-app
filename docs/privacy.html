Evolving AI · Harvard, October 8–10, 2026

Privacy policy

Effective September 22, 2026. This policy covers the Evolving AI website and iPhone and iPad app used to prepare for the gathering.

Information we collect

When you submit the form, we collect your name, email address, affiliation, selected topics, written questions, and any work details or gathering goals you choose to provide. We also store discussion posts, content reports, block choices, and any debrief responses you provide. A salted hash of your personal access answer is stored to let you return to your reading; the answer itself is not stored in the Sheet. The invitation passcode is checked when you submit.

How we use and share it

Organizers use submissions to prepare reading lists, discussion groups, and question sheets for this gathering. Attendees who have submitted can see other attendees' names, affiliations, questions, and discussion posts in the discussion area. Organizers can access the full submissions, including email addresses. We do not sell attendee information or use it for advertising or analytics.

Where it is stored

Submitted information is stored in an organizer-controlled Google Sheet through Google Apps Script. Your draft and reading progress may remain in your browser or on your device so you can resume. The invitation passcode and personal access answer are held only while the page or app is open; opening an updated version clears copies stored by older releases. The app copies only its public address when you choose “Copy app link.” Older personal links may contain a readable copy of a submission; treat any such link as private. The website loads fonts from Adobe and Google; opening a linked reading takes you to its publisher. Those services may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address.

Retention and your choices

We will remove attendee submissions, discussion posts, reports, block choices, and debrief responses from the active Google Sheet by November 10, 2026, one month after the gathering ends. Copies on your own device remain until you clear the website's storage or remove the app. Google may retain service backups under its own policies.

To ask to see, correct, or delete your information sooner, email ewang@benchmark.com. You can also use that address for privacy questions.
