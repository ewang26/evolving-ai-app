Support

For help with the Evolving AI website or iPhone and iPad app, email ewang@benchmark.com.

Saving or loading your reading

After entering your details, continue through the form to save your reading to the organizers' Sheet. A successful save is confirmed in the app. If it says your work is saved only on this device, reconnect and use Retry. To load a prior submission, enter the same email address and favorite AI model you used originally, then tap “Load my reading.”

“Copy app link” copies the public app address. Your answers are loaded separately after you enter your email and favorite AI model. Keep any older personal link containing #s= private because it may include a readable copy of your answers.

Access problems

New attendees need the invitation passcode from their invitation email. If you cannot access an existing submission, or have forgotten the personal answer you used, contact the organizer. Include your name and the email address used for the submission; do not email your personal answer.

Discussion

Discussion is available after your submission has been saved to the Sheet. If a page does not load or a post does not appear, check your connection, refresh, and try again. Contact support if the problem continues.

Use “Report content” beside a post or question to flag offensive material for organizer review. “Block attendee” hides that person's posts and questions from your view. Email ewang@benchmark.com for urgent concerns or to undo a block.
