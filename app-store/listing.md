The app collects a name, email address, affiliation, topic choices, written questions, optional work details, discussion posts, and debrief responses. These are tied to an attendee's submission. The organizer can see the full submission; signed-in attendees can see names, affiliations, questions, and posts in discussion. No advertising or analytics SDK is included. Review the precise App Store Connect data-type choices against these facts before saving the questionnaire.
